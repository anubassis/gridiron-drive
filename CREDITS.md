# Gridiron Drive — 3D asset credits

All models and textures listed here are used under licenses that permit use and redistribution in a public web game. No NC, ND, or unclear-license assets, and no ripped or copyrighted game/league content (no NFL, Madden, or Poki assets). Mixamo was not used.

Built-in procedural players, field markings, goalposts, and the stadium bowl remain as a fallback if these files fail to load.

## Characters

**Kenney — Mini Characters (2.0)**  
License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)  
Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters  
Files: `assets/characters/character-male-a.glb`, `character-male-c.glb`  
Used as the rigged, animated on-field players (idle, sprint, throw, catch, fall, celebrate, and related clips). Models are retinted to each team’s colors; a simple helmet and jersey-number decal are added in-engine. Attribution to Kenney is appreciated but not required.
